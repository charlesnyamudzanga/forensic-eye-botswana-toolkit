# Forensic Readiness Checklist
> Forensic Eye Botswana (Pty) Ltd — Law Enforcement Support Series

## Purpose
This checklist ensures that an organisation or law enforcement unit is
prepared to collect, preserve, and present digital evidence before an
incident occurs.

---

## 1. Policy & Governance
- [ ] Digital forensics policy is documented and approved
- [ ] Incident response plan includes digital evidence procedures
- [ ] Data retention policy is defined and enforced
- [ ] Chain of custody policy is in place

## 2. Personnel Readiness
- [ ] At least one trained digital forensics officer is designated
- [ ] Staff are aware of evidence preservation responsibilities
- [ ] External forensics support contacts are documented (e.g. Forensic Eye Botswana)

## 3. Tools & Equipment
- [ ] Write-blockers are available and tested
- [ ] Forensic imaging tools are licensed and up to date (e.g. FTK, Autopsy)
- [ ] Hash verification tools are available (see hash_verify.py in this repo)
- [ ] Secure evidence storage media is available (encrypted drives)

## 4. Network & Logging
- [ ] System and network logs are enabled and retained for minimum 90 days
- [ ] SIEM or log aggregation is configured
- [ ] Time synchronisation (NTP) is enforced across all systems

## 5. Legal Preparedness
- [ ] Legal counsel familiar with Botswana Cyber Crime Act is identified
- [ ] Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT) contacts are documented
- [ ] Evidence admissibility requirements are understood by the team
