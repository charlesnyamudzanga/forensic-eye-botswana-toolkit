## Module 1: What is Digital Forensics?
Digital forensics is the process of collecting, preserving, analysing,
and presenting digital evidence in a manner that is legally admissible.

**Core Principles:**
- **Integrity** — Evidence must not be altered during collection or analysis
- **Authenticity** — Evidence must be proven to be what it claims to be
- **Chain of Custody** — Every person who handles evidence must be documented
- **Repeatability** — Forensic findings must be reproducible by an independent examiner
