# Botswana Cyber Law Reference Guide
> Forensic Eye Botswana (Pty) Ltd — Legal Compliance Series

## Overview
This reference guide summarises the key legislation governing cybercrime,
digital evidence, and data protection in Botswana, relevant to law
enforcement and digital forensics practitioners.

---

## 1. Cybercrime and Computer Related Crimes Act (CCRCA) — 2007
- Criminalises unauthorised access to computer systems
- Covers interception of data and computer fraud
- Provides for search and seizure of digital devices
- Penalties: fines and imprisonment up to 10 years depending on offence

**Key Sections for Investigators:**
- Section 4: Unauthorised access
- Section 7: Interception of data
- Section 9: Computer fraud
- Section 15: Powers of search and seizure

---

## 2. Data Protection Act (DPA) — 2018
- Governs collection, storage, and processing of personal data
- Establishes the Information and Data Authority (IDA)
- Requires lawful basis for processing personal data
- Impacts how investigators handle victim and suspect data

**Key Obligations:**
- Data must be collected for a specified, lawful purpose
- Retention periods must be defined
- Data subjects have rights of access and correction

---

## 3. Electronic Records (Evidence) Act — 2014
- Provides for admissibility of electronic records as evidence in court
- Sets requirements for authentication of digital evidence
- Hash values and audit trails support admissibility

**Admissibility Requirements:**
- Evidence must be shown to be unaltered
- Chain of custody must be documented
- Forensic examiner may be required to testify

---

## 4. Mutual Legal Assistance (MLA)
- Botswana is signatory to SADC MLA frameworks
- Enables cross-border digital evidence requests
- Contact: Attorney General's Chambers, Gaborone
