---

## 🎯 Purpose

This toolkit assists:

1. **Victims**: Validating IMEI numbers and generating standardized incident reports.
2. **Law Enforcement**: Verifying device integrity and formatting evidence for the SADC Cybercrime Database.
3. **Developers**: Open-source IMEI validation algorithms (Luhn check) for integration into larger forensic tools.

## 🌍 Why This Matters for SADC

Mobile phone theft is one of the most common crimes across the SADC region. Botswana, Zimbabwe, Zambia, South Africa, and Tanzania all report high volumes of mobile device theft — yet:

- Most victims don't know their IMEI number
- Police lack standardized evidence documentation
- Cross-border tracking is nearly impossible without proper IMEI records
- Courts reject poorly documented digital evidence

**PhoneRecover-SADC bridges this gap** — giving victims and law enforcement a free, offline, court-ready tool built specifically for the African context.
