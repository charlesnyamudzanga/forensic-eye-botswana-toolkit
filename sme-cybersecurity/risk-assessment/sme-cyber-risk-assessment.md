## Step 2: Threat Identification
Common threats facing Botswana SMEs:

- **Phishing** — Fake emails impersonating BURS, FNBB, or suppliers
- **Ransomware** — Files encrypted and held for payment
- **Mobile Money Fraud** — Fake Orange Money / MyZaka notifications
- **Insider Threats** — Disgruntled or careless staff
- **Weak Passwords** — Default or reused credentials
- **Unpatched Software** — Outdated Windows, accounting software

## Step 4: Controls & Mitigations

| Risk | Recommended Control |
|---|---|
| Phishing | Staff awareness training, email filtering |
| Ransomware | Offline backups, endpoint protection |
| Mobile money fraud | Transaction limits, staff verification procedures |
| Insider threat | Role-based access, audit logs |
| Weak passwords | Password manager, MFA enforcement |
| Unpatched software | Monthly patch schedule |
