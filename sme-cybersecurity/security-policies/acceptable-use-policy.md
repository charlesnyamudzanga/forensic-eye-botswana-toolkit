## 1. Purpose
This Acceptable Use Policy defines how staff may use company technology
resources including computers, internet, email, and mobile devices. It
protects the organisation from security breaches, legal liability, and
reputational damage.

---

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties
who access company systems or data.

---

## 3. Acceptable Use

### 3.1 Internet
- ✅ Use the internet for work-related tasks
- ✅ Access approved cloud services (e.g. Google Workspace, Microsoft 365)
- ✅ Research relevant to your role

### 3.2 Email
- ✅ Send professional communications
- ✅ Use company email for business purposes
- ✅ Report suspicious emails to IT immediately

### 3.3 Devices
- ✅ Use company devices for work tasks
- ✅ Lock your screen when stepping away
- ✅ Report lost or stolen devices within 1 hour

---

## 4. Unacceptable Use

### 4.1 Internet
- ❌ Visiting illegal, gambling, or adult websites
- ❌ Downloading unlicensed software or pirated content
- ❌ Using company internet for personal business

### 4.2 Email
- ❌ Sending confidential data to personal email accounts
- ❌ Opening unexpected attachments without IT approval
- ❌ Clicking links in unsolicited emails

### 4.3 Devices & Data
- ❌ Installing unauthorised software
- ❌ Sharing passwords with colleagues
- ❌ Connecting personal USB drives without IT approval
- ❌ Taking company data offsite without authorisation

---

## 5. Password Requirements
- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and symbol
- Must not be reused from previous 10 passwords
- Must be changed every 90 days
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) required for all cloud accounts

---

## 6. Consequences of Violation
Violations of this policy may result in:
- Verbal or written warning
- Suspension of system access
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination
- Referral to law enforcement where criminal activity is involved
