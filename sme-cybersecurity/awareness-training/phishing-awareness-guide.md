---

## What is Phishing?
Phishing is when a criminal sends a fake message pretending to be a
trusted organisation — like BURS, your bank, or your boss — to trick
you into clicking a link, opening a file, or giving away your password.

---

## 🚩 Warning Signs — Stop Before You Click

### Fake Email Red Flags
- Sender email doesn't match the organisation (e.g. `burs-refund@gmail.com`)
- Urgent language: *"Your account will be closed in 24 hours"*
- Unexpected attachments (especially .zip, .exe, .docx)
- Links that don't match the real website
- Poor spelling and grammar

### Fake SMS / WhatsApp Red Flags
- Unknown number claiming to be your bank or Orange Money
- Asking you to send money to "verify" your account
- Links to websites that look almost right (e.g. `orangemoney-bw.net`)
- Claiming you won a prize you never entered

---

## 🇧🇼 Real Examples Seen in Botswana

| Scam Type | How It Looks |
|---|---|
| BURS Tax Refund | Email saying you have a tax refund — click to claim |
| FNB / Stanbic Alert | SMS saying your account is locked — verify now |
| Orange Money Prize | WhatsApp message saying you won P5,000 |
| CEO Fraud | Email from "your boss" asking you to buy gift cards urgently |
| Fake Invoice | Supplier email with changed bank account details |

---

## ✅ What To Do

1. **STOP** — Do not click, download, or reply
2. **CHECK** — Call the sender directly using a known number
3. **REPORT** — Tell your IT person or manager immediately
4. **DELETE** — Remove the message from your inbox

---

## ❌ What NOT To Do

- Never enter your password on a page you reached by clicking a link
- Never send money based on an email or SMS alone
- Never open attachments you were not expecting
- Never share your OTP with anyone — ever

---

## Quick Test — Is This Phishing?

> *"Dear Customer, your FNB account has been suspended due to suspicious
> activity. Click here to verify: http://fnb-verify-bw.com/login"*

**Answer: YES — This is phishing.**
- Real banks never ask you to verify via a link in an SMS
- The URL is not the official FNB website (fnb.co.bw)
